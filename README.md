# Aorta

Your body, in your ears.

Aorta is a client-side Fabric mod that gives your player a heartbeat and breath
you can hear. It doesn't play a loop: it runs a small physiological model each
tick, driven by what your player is actually going through, and synthesizes each
lub-dub and each breath from that model. Nothing is a recording — every sound is
generated by [`tools/gen_sounds.py`](tools/gen_sounds.py), a handful of kilobytes
of numbers with an explanation attached.

The heart speeds up when you sprint, thumps when something hostile is near, and
races as your air runs out underwater. The breath deepens with exertion and turns
ragged when you're hurt. Stand still in the light and it settles back toward a
slow, quiet rest.

It is meant to sit alongside the rest of the suite: *Foley* makes the world's
sound, *Earshot* decides how you hear it, *Yak VC* carries other players' voices,
*Gore* shows the blood, and *Aorta* makes your own body audible.

## How it works

Every client tick, Aorta samples the player's state:

- health and max health
- food and saturation
- air supply (drowning)
- sprinting, moving, falling
- nearby hostiles (threat)
- light level and depth below the surface

That state is distilled into a [`BodyInputs`](src/main/java/dev/lume/aorta/body/BodyInputs.java)
record and fed through a closed-form [`BodyModel`](src/main/java/dev/lume/aorta/body/BodyModel.java),
which produces a heart rate, a breath rate, a breath depth, and a heart force.
Two pure, integer-tick [`HeartbeatClock`](src/main/java/dev/lume/aorta/body/HeartbeatClock.java)
and [`BreathClock`](src/main/java/dev/lume/aorta/body/BreathClock.java) turn those
rates into a stream of one-shot sounds played through the client's `SoundManager`.

The model, the clocks, and the config are all pure and unit-tested; the only place
Minecraft types appear is the thin [`BodyDriver`](src/client/java/dev/lume/aorta/client/BodyDriver.java)
that samples the game and plays the sounds.

Each breath and beat is baked as a few variants, and the engine picks one at
random per play, so repeats never sound identical.

## Config

`config/aorta.json` (written on first launch):

```json
{
  "version": 4,
  "enabled": true,
  "heartbeatVolume": 0.2,
  "breathingVolume": 0.2
}
```

## Settings

Press **F9** (rebindable under Controls → Miscellaneous) or run `/aorta menu` to
open a small screen with a volume slider and a heartbeat on/off toggle. The slider
previews live and is saved when the screen closes.

## Commands

- `/aorta menu` — open the volume/on-off screen (also F9)
- `/aorta status` — the live heart and breath rates
- `/aorta simulate` — force a beat
- `/aorta reload` — re-read the config

## Building

```sh
./gradlew build
```

Requires JDK 25 and a warm Loom cache. The game data run dirs live in
`~/aorta-run/<name>`; override with `-Paorta.runDir=<dir>`.
